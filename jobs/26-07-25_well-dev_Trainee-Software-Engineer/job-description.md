## Role Overview

**WellDev** is a technology-driven software development company committed to delivering cutting-edge solutions. As we continue to grow, we are looking for exceptional talents wishing to boost their career. Freshers are encouraged to apply in this position. You will be hired as a Trainee Software Engineer which is a full time permanent employment and will gain exposure across different areas of SDLC. This program is specially designed to boost up the career in Software Industry just after graduation from university without any software development experience.

This role has evolved significantly over the past two years. We are seeking candidates who can think clearly, verify AI-generated output rigorously, and adapt their skills as quickly as the industry changes. You will join a team of exceptional developers working in a fast-paced environment to deliver world-class software.

## Responsibilities

- Learn new technologies, tools and frameworks to build a foundation in software development life cycle (SDLC).
- Show dedication and a willingness to go the extra mile to achieve goals.
- Assist in creating flowcharts, layouts and documentation to identify requirements and solutions.
- Collaborate with senior engineers and team members to contribute to projects effectively.
- Use AI-assisted development tools effectively to increase productivity, while retaining full ownership of correctness, security, and code quality.
- Adapt across the stack as project needs evolve. Expect to work across multiple languages, frameworks, and domains during your first two years rather than specialize narrowly.
- Critically evaluate AI-generated code, suggestions, and design proposals, and raise concerns when they are incorrect or suboptimal.
- Continuously update your skills as tooling, models, and best practices evolve.
- Participate in discussions, brainstorming sessions and code reviews to enhance learning.
- Develop problem-solving skills and tackle challenges with a positive mindset.
- Proactively seek feedback and focus on personal growth.
- Actively learn and grow in areas like security practices, phishing awareness and company policies.
- Understand and adapt to the company culture, values and workflows.
- Exhibit reliability by showing up on time and maintaining consistent attendance.
- Participate in training sessions and workshops to enhance skills and knowledge.

## Requirements

- Demonstrated ability to learn unfamiliar languages, frameworks, or tools quickly. Candidates should be prepared to discuss a concrete example during the interview, such as a project where they learned the stack while building it.
- Working fluently with AI coding assistants (such as Copilot, Cursor, or Claude Code), including a clear understanding of their limitations and the ability to verify their output.
- Strong fundamentals in algorithms, data structures, and systems thinking, sufficient to debug, review, and correct AI-generated code rather than accept it uncritically.
- Ability to decompose ambiguous problems into precise specifications. Strong reasoning and specification skills are weighted more heavily than coding throughput.
- Ability to read source code, RFCs, and primary documentation independently, rather than relying solely on tutorials.
- Exposure to modern backend communication patterns (REST, gRPC, event-driven architectures), with a willingness to learn whatever the project demands.
- A firm grasp of object-oriented analysis and design.
- Effective and professional interpersonal communication skills in English and Bangla are mandatory.
- Ability to tackle new challenges, perform proactively, and take ownership within the scope.
