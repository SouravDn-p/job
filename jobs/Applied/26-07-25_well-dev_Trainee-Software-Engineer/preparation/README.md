# Preparation — WellDev, Trainee Software Engineer

## The 60-second version

WellDev runs **four stages**: an **MCQ test**, a **one-hour technical interview**, a **second
technical interview with an HR half**, and a short **COO round**. The MCQ is the gate that fails most
people, and it is **broad, not deep** — DSA, DBMS, OOP, networking, REST, JavaScript and maths
reasoning all in one paper.

A former WellDev interviewer said publicly that most of their questions are easy and any fresh
graduate should have a basic idea. What they actually screen for is who stays composed on the
questions they have never seen. **That is a breadth-and-nerve test, and it is preparable.**

## ⚠️ Flags — deal with these before you sit anything

1. **The test is ONSITE.** The 2026 posting says so. Candidates in 2024 described a *remote* test on
   Quilgo with screen share and camera. **This changed** — plan to travel to the Dhaka office.
2. **The JD's tentative date was 31 July 2026.** If your invitation email says something else, **the
   email wins.** Re-read it tonight.
3. **The recruitment-process PDF linked in the JD needs a login** — I could not read it. Open it
   yourself; it may state the test duration and the number of rounds.
4. **Ask whether there is negative marking.** It decides whether you guess on every question or not.
5. **Your CGPA is unverified** — recorded as 3.59, an older source says 3.42. Fresher pipelines check
   transcripts, and WellDev gates on academic certificates. Confirm against the IUBAT transcript.
6. **JavaScript leads their reported topic list and you are a Python engineer.** It is the biggest
   gap between this paper and your CV. Session 8 exists for it — do not let it slip.

Each stage folder holds the same four things: `plan.md` (the schedule), `preparation.md` (everything
to learn, in one linear file), `listen/` (read-aloud scripts), and `question-bank/` (the questions,
plus an `INDEX.md` manifest).

## Confidence

**High on the process, medium on the paper.** Two first-hand candidate reports name the rounds, the
platform and dozens of questions — see [`research.md`](research.md). But those reports are from
**2024**, and this JD has been visibly rewritten for the AI era. The topic list is the best evidence
available, not a guarantee.

**There is no leaked answer key here.** Of the 126 MCQs, all but two were written by me to match the
reported style. Do not walk in expecting to recognise the paper.
