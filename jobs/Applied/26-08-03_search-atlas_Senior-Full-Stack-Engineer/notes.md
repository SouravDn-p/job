required. AI power user, not open to learning."** Most candidates cannot evidence this. He can:
Claude Code and Cursor in daily production use, plus hand-written `CONTEXT.md` / `CLAUDE.md`
architectural constitutions per repo and the formal `ai_rules.md` engineering constitution on LookUp

"Pressure-test product specs, not merely execute them" is answered by SafeTag (§7.9) and the
ProspectLead `Context.md` product spec (§7.3).

## Knockouts — deal with these before applying

1. **Timezone: EST or GMT+1.** Dhaka is UTC+6. **GMT+1 is a 5-hour offset — workable** (their
   morning is his afternoon/evening). **EST is UTC-5, an 11-hour offset — brutal**, roughly a
   7pm–3am Dhaka shift. Find out which of the two this role actually is *before* investing in the
   application. This is the single biggest risk on an otherwise excellent match.
2. **5+ years required; he has ~2 years professional** (Softvence from Aug 2025, internship Feb–Jul
   2024). This is a genuine gap and must not be papered over. The counter-argument is scope, not
   duration: eight production backends in eleven months, promoted junior → senior inside that window.
3. **Contractor vs employee.** Listed as "employee" at an hourly rate — clarify how a Bangladesh-based
   hire is engaged and paid before accepting.
